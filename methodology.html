03Known limits

  • Announced ≠ realized. Layoff counts are announcement figures; some include attrition/unfilled roles (noted per event).
  • Announced hiring ≠ actual hiring. Most hiring is never announced; use the filings-based headcount series and the implied-gross-hiring metric (net change + announced layoffs) instead.
  • Net ≠ gross. Year-end headcount deltas mix hiring, layoffs, attrition and M&A across all areas of a company.
  • Coverage is announcement-driven. Small rolling cuts are undercounted except where estimated (Google 2026). Cross-check industry totals against Layoffs.fyi/TrueUp/Skillsyncer, which count differently.
  • Buyout offers ≠ separations. Voluntary programs (Microsoft 8,750 → 60,000) count announced offers; acceptance rates noted per event.
  • Private-lab headcounts are estimates (single dated media points, dashed on charts); public companies use 10-K/10-Q counts at fiscal year-end (year-ends differ by company).
  • Amazon's % figures refer to its ~350k corporate base, not the 1.5M+ total workforce.
  • LinkedIn profile counts are not used as a proxy — not officially time-stamped and methodologically unstable.
