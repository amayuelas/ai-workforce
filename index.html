06How robust is this tracker?

Every event carries a confidence tier: high = company-stated (newsroom, CEO memo, SEC filing) · medium = reputable wire reporting of unconfirmed figures · low = single secondary source / estimate. Known limits — announced ≠ realized, layoffs and hiring plans are different units, private-lab headcounts are estimates, and fiscal years end on different dates — are documented in docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
