02Announced layoffs vs implied actual hiring

Per company, latest fiscal year: red = announced layoffs, green = implied gross hiring = net headcount change (10-K) + announced layoffs. Announced hiring plans look tiny because most hiring is never announced — the filings-based estimate is the real number. Year-end net change mixes both plus attrition and M&A across all areas of the company, which is why it differs from either bar.

07How robust is this tracker?

Every event carries a confidence tier: high = company-stated (newsroom, CEO memo, SEC filing) · medium = reputable wire reporting of unconfirmed figures · low = single secondary source. Limits: announced ≠ realized; announced hiring ≠ actual hiring (use 01/02); private-lab headcounts are estimates; small unannounced cuts are undercounted. Full notes: docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
