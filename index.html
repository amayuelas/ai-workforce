WorkforceBench

As AI reshapes the industry: are the main tech companies and AI labs net hiring or net shrinking? Measured two ways — announced events (layoffs negative, hirings positive) and reported headcount from SEC filings (the reliable net signal — most hiring is never announced). 08How robust is this tracker?

Every event carries a confidence tier: high = company-stated (newsroom, CEO memo, SEC filing) · medium = reputable wire reporting of unconfirmed figures · low = single secondary source. Limits: announced ≠ realized; announced hiring ≠ actual hiring (use 01–03); private-lab headcounts are estimates; small unannounced cuts are undercounted. Full notes, coverage check vs external trackers, and known limits: methodology & coverage page · docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
